Technical clarifications

At approximately 19:08 in the full recording (about 4:08 into Part 2), negative quantities are excluded by the original low-stock query because it requires quantity > 0. That query counts quantities 1 through 5; “not zero” alone is not the reason.

The company/login example near 34:39 describes a possible future direction. The application uses shared inventory. Different usernames alone do not separate organizations or authorize access to separate inventories. Barcode lookup, serialized-equipment tracking, and broader search fields remain proposals.
